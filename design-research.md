# Design Research & Master Architecture

## 1. Master Reference: MotionSites AI — 3D Jack Portfolio
- **URL**: `https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=3d-jack-portfolio-hero`
- **Role in Project**: Master architectural reference for page sequence, scroll rhythm, interaction model, and component layout.
- **Core Philosophy**: "Replicate the experience architecture, not the original branding."

## 2. Page Architecture & Section Order
1. **01. HERO**: Full-viewport immersive introduction. Centered studio pedestal composition, oversized typography occluded behind visual, staged element reveal, bottom metadata bar, and clear CTAs.
2. **02. MARQUEE**: Dual opposing continuous horizontal strips driven by scroll velocity. Replaced random stock tiles with authentic multimedia portfolio frames (Documentary, E-Magazine, Infographic, Talking Head, Red Bull Stratos).
3. **03. ABOUT**: Spacious editorial narrative. Centered title, scroll-driven character/word opacity reveal, generous negative space, bordered by 4 art-directed multimedia objects (Cinema Camera, Film Reel, Editorial Magazine, Waveform/Timeline).
4. **04. TÔI LÀM GÌ (Capabilities)**: Minimal editorial list layout (NOT cards). Massive stylized numbers, discipline title, concise craft description, thin hairline dividers.
5. **05. DỰ ÁN (Selected Work)**: Sticky stacking card physics with scroll-linked scaling and asymmetric 40/60 editorial media presentation.
6. **06. LIÊN HỆ (Footer)**: Atmospheric twilight grounding, oversized backdrop typography, warm personal gratitude statement, direct contact channels.

## 3. Supporting References & Inspiration
- **Minimal Gallery**: Restrained editorial typography contrast, asymmetric magazine margins, disciplined white space.
- **Kage & Refero**: Clean horizontal discipline indexing with thin border dividers.
- **21st.dev & Motion Primitives**: Velocity-responsive marquee, spring-lag cursor parallax, sticky card scale interpolations.
- **3Dicons**: Tactile studio lighting, soft material textures, ambient occlusion without artificial neon gloss.
